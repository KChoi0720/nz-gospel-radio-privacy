Privacy Policy

NZ Christian Radio (iOS App) · Last updated: 26 November 2026

In short: NZ Christian Radio does not collect your personal information, keeps your favourites and settings solely on your device, and never sells or shares data for advertising.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

NZ Christian Radio is designed with your privacy in mind. The app does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or location, and does not collect personal information.

2. Local Device Storage

Your favourite radio stations and app settings are stored locally on your device only. This data is not uploaded to our servers or shared with third parties, and it is removed if you delete the app.

3. Radio Streams & Third-Party Services

When you play a station, the app streams audio directly from third-party radio station servers. As with any internet audio stream, those third-party broadcasters or streaming providers may receive standard technical connection details (such as your IP address and device user agent) required to deliver the audio stream, governed by their own privacy policies.

The app may also use Google Firebase to load up-to-date station information (such as station names, stream URLs, and logos). Firebase processes only the technical request data necessary to deliver that station list.

4. No Advertising or Data Selling

We do not sell, rent, or trade any user data, and we do not share any information with third parties for advertising, marketing, or cross-app tracking purposes.

5. Children's Privacy

Because NZ Christian Radio does not collect personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

If our practices change in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy and revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or the NZ Christian Radio app, please contact us at nzchristianradio@gmail.com.
